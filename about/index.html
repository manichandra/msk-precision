Our Story

From a vision of precision engineering excellence to becoming a trusted partner in manufacturing, consulting, and technical education.

Founded in 2019

MSK Precision Engineering Group was established in 2019 in the vibrant industrial landscape of Telangana, India. What began as a vision to bridge the gap between traditional manufacturing and modern precision engineering has evolved into a comprehensive engineering solutions provider.

Telangana, India

Our Mission

To deliver world-class precision manufacturing, innovative engineering consulting, and comprehensive technical training services that empower businesses and individuals to achieve excellence in engineering. We are committed to advancing the precision engineering ecosystem through quality, innovation, and continuous learning.

Key Highlights

Precision Manufacturing

State-of-the-art CNC machining with ±5 µm tolerances

Engineering Consulting

Advanced CFD, FEA, and AI-driven design optimization

Technical Training

Comprehensive programs bridging academia and industry

Quality Assurance

ISO 9001:2015 certified processes and CMM inspection

Our Vision
